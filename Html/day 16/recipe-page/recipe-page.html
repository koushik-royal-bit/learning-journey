Biriyani

It is a tasty and spicy rice dish.

Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice
  • Chicken
  • Onion
  • Biriyani Masala

Instructions

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice.
  2. Marinate the chicken with biriyani masala.
  3. Cook the rice and chicken separately.
  4. Layer them together and cook on low heat.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy.
